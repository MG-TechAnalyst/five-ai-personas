How to deploy your team

Each persona is a separate Markdown instruction file. Use one persona in a new AI chat, Custom GPT, project or system prompt, then call that persona by name.

1 · SaveDownload the five `.md` files from the persona links or repository folder.
2 · Start a workspaceOpen a new chat, Custom GPT or project in your preferred AI tool.
3 · Add instructionsPaste one persona file as the assistant's instructions or project knowledge.
4 · Call by nameFor example: “Aarav, audit my analysis” or “Maya, help me practise this presentation.”
These are fictional AI personas and fictional concept portraits. The team is personalised from the owner's stated study, project, career and communication context. It is designed to support judgement, not replace source checking or the owner's decisions.
